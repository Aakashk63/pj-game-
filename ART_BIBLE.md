# ARCHITECTURA: ART BIBLE & STYLE SPECIFICATION

## 1. Visual Identity & Metaphor
Architectura teaches distributed systems and backend infrastructure through a living, tactile, isometric corporate campus on a floating tech island.

Abstract software concepts map directly to tangible physical elements:
- **Client**: Visitors and employees commuting to the company with requests.
- **Server**: The Company building, housing desks, processing units, and personnel.
- **Request / Response**: Network packets physically floating between commuters and the building.
- **Authentication**: Gatekeeper guard checking identity credentials at the perimeter.
- **Authorization**: Keycards and biometric room readers permitting entry to specific rooms.
- **Load / Traffic**: Commuter volume filling hallways, queues, and turnstiles.
- **Failure / Crash**: Structural stress, building wobble, alarm lights, smoke, and physical breakdown.

## 2. Color Palette & Comic Strip Styling
All colors are curated for a premium, friendly cartoon comic aesthetic:
- **Sky & Atmosphere**: Sky blue (`#7DD3FC` to `#E0F2FE`), soft horizon fog (`#CBD5E1`), bright warm sunlight (`#FFFDF5`).
- **Success / Validated**: Emerald Green (`#2ECC71`) for checkmarks, granted access, healthy status.
- **Failure / Breach**: Alert Red (`#E74C3C`) for unauthorized breaches, server overload, jammed turnstiles.
- **Highlight / Terms**: Vibrant Yellow (`#F1C40F`) for term banners, glowing attention rings, and callouts.
- **Headers & Authority**: Deep Navy (`#1B2A4A`) for security guard uniforms, headers, and framing elements.
- **Ground & Campus**: Terracotta & cream pavers (`#A65D46`, `#EDE1D1`), dark asphalt (`#2D3748`), grass accents (`#3B7A57`).
- **Corporate Building**: Concrete stone (`#A0AAB2`), warm commercial brick (`#8D6E63`), reflective blue glass bays (`#38BDF8`), illuminated warm windows (`#FEF08A`).

## 3. Characters & Proportions
- **Security Guard**: Peaked cap with gold badge, navy uniform (`#1B2A4A`), friendly mustache, warm skin tones, crisp posture with stop/welcome gestures.
- **Employees**: Stylized casual attire (t-shirts, jeans, leather jackets), backpacks, color-coded ID lanyards, bobbing walking cycle.
- **Strangers / Untrusted**: Kid-friendly cartoon hooded visitor (`#334155`), sneaky stance, looking for unattended items.

## 4. Comic Book Storyboard UI
- **Numbered Panel Badges**: Every story moment displays a numbered badge (`1`, `2`, `3`...) matching the comic reference.
- **Strict Text Brevity**:
  - Captions: **Max 12 words** per beat.
  - Speech Bubbles: **Max 8 words** per bubble.
- **Consequence Highlighting**: Every choice triggers a physical reaction, highlighting weak points with pulsing rings, 3D arrow pointers, and concise feedback.

## 5. Lighting, Camera & Polish
- **Camera Angles**: Isometric perspective with 38° FOV for standard dioramas; smooth GSAP dolly zoom for close-ups and pedestal pullbacks.
- **Lighting**: Warm key directional sunlight with crisp diagonal shadows, soft ambient fill, and gentle post-processing bloom.
- **Tactile Effects**: Procedural screen shake on alarms, 3D paper confetti bursts on success, floating particle clouds on placement, glowing Bezier request packets.
